Project review performed while packaging:
- Reviewed the profile-to-settings mapping and default profile values.
- Added input-rate clamping (30 CPS for regular profiles; 40 CPS maximum for TPS mode).
- Added a dedicated input thread with short sleeps rather than a busy-spin loop, plus a 350 ms activation grace period.
- Added atomic run/stop state and a shutdown flag; the app joins its worker thread on close.
- Avoided unwrap() on mouse initialisation, click injection, settings loading/saving, and input-thread startup.
- Added unit tests for safe rate clamping, the TPS defaults and keybind activation defaults.
